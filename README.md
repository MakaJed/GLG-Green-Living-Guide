# GLG Project
Green Living Guide Game

An educational pixel-art RPG designed to promote environmental awareness, eco-friendly habits, and sustainability through interactive storytelling and turn-based combat.

---

## 📖 Overview

**Green Living Guide (GLG)** is a 2D RPG that combines character-driven narrative choices with monster-taming and battle mechanics. Players explore vibrant environments, engage in story encounters highlighting real-world environmental issues (such as littering and pollution), and participate in battles featuring unique creatures like **Cubble**, **Meowmblebee**, and **Shardlass**.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Story & Dialogue System:** Interactive conversations with characters like Rheeyu that emphasize environmental protection, waste management, and sustainable practices.
- **Turn-Based Battle System:** Classic battle interface complete with player stats, special skills, items, and strategic options.
- **Eco-Mode & Mechanics:** Unique gameplay elements integrated into combat and world exploration that encourage eco-friendly strategy.
- **Retro Pixel-Art Graphics:** Charming pixelated character avatars, battle sprites, and lush natural backgrounds.

## 🕹️ Controls & Navigation

- **Party / Stats:** View creature HP, MP, EXP, and current party lineup.
- **Skill / Item:** Execute elemental skills or utilize consumable items during encounters.
- **Eco-Mode:** Toggle special eco-friendly enhancements during battle.
- **Run / Defend:** Mitigate incoming damage or escape wild encounters.
